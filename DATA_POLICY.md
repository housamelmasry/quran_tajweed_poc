# Data Usage Policy

## 1. Overview

This project includes code for analyzing Quran recitation (Tajweed) using audio processing techniques.

⚠️ IMPORTANT:
Audio files (reference or user recordings) are NOT included in this repository.

---

## 2. Audio Files Policy

### 2.1 Reference Audio (Quran Recitation)

- Reference Quran recitations may be subject to copyright or usage restrictions.
- Users are responsible for obtaining audio from legal and permissible sources.
- The project author does NOT provide or distribute Quran audio files.

Allowed usage:

- Personal learning
- Research and experimentation

Not guaranteed:

- Commercial usage rights (depends on source of audio)

---

### 2.2 User Recordings

- User-generated recordings remain the property of the user.
- The system processes audio locally (no automatic upload or sharing).
- Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to use and store recordings.
